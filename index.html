Ours oceans are an underwater wonderland

Ocean danceen

Sharks the size of small boats, fish disquising themselves as seaweed and cephalopds performing dazzling light shows all wxist within our oceans here in Australia. Beneath our sun-soaked waves of our coastline lies a world of wonder and nystery. As one of the world's most biodiverse marine habitats, nature has crafted creatures so extraordinary they seem to belong in the pages of a fantasy novel. Australia's seas are home to some of the most wonderous and bizarre creatures out there.
